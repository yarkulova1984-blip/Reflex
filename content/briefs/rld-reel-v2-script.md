Fact-check basis:
- Lymph has no central pump like the heart; it is moved by skeletal muscle, breathing and the rhythmic contraction of lymph vessel walls (textbook physiology).
- RLD = Reflexology Lymph Drainage, a specific reflexology technique (Sally Kay; research with Cardiff Metropolitan University). Main study: Whatley, Street, Kay & Harris 2016, uncontrolled feasibility study, 26 women with breast-cancer-related lymphoedema; arm volume reduced; the authors say the results cannot be attributed to RLD because of the design, and an RCT is needed. => Evidence is early and small: no promises of reduced swelling, stronger immunity or recovery.
- Reflex areas are reflexology concepts, not anatomy.
