Every foot has 26 bones and 33 joints. In reflexology, it also holds a map.

Foot reflexology is based on the idea that specific areas of the feet correspond to different parts of the body. Nurse and physiotherapist Eunice Ingham developed these foot reflex maps in the 1930s, and they still guide reflexology practice today.

In a session, that map becomes a focused, hands-on treatment designed to help you slow down and relax.

Reflexology is a complementary practice and does not replace medical care.
